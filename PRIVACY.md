# Privacy policy

**PS2 BIOS & Emulator Assistant**  
Effective date: 14 September 2026  
Applies to the Android app with application ID `com.allps2bios.assistant`.

This document describes the app as implemented in this repository. It is intended as a Play Console privacy-policy draft. Host it at a stable HTTPS URL before listing the app.

## Summary

The app is an on-device utility. It does not require an account and does not include a first-party server.

## Data the app processes

When you choose **Select BIOS File**, the app reads the file you pick in order to show its name, size, MD5 checksum, and SHA-1 checksum. That work happens on the device.

The app does not upload the selected file and does not transmit BIOS contents to a developer server.

## Data collection

This version of the app does not include analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or social SDKs.

The Android system file picker may be provided by the operating system. The app does not receive a copy of your whole storage.

## Website link

The About screen can open https://allps2bios.com/ in your browser. That site is outside the app. Its own privacy policy applies once the browser is open. Opening the link does not send your BIOS file.

## Permissions

The release app is not intended to request storage or internet permission. Debug builds may request internet so Flutter developer tooling can connect.

## Children

The app is a general utility and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If the app later adds networking, accounts, or third-party SDKs, this policy and the Play Data safety form must be updated before those builds are shipped.
